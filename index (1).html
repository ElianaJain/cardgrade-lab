The idea

Grading demand can outgrow the number of trained graders, and manual inspection is slow and varies over a long shift. This project asks which parts of inspection a machine can measure reliably, and which still need a person.

The rule I’m building around: AI prepares and explains, a human decides.

  1. Capture
    Consistent lighting, high resolution, several angles.
  2. Measure
    Computer vision scores centering, corners, edges, and surface.
  3. Explain
    A vision-language model flags anomalies and writes a short rationale a person can verify.
  4. Route
    Confident, low-value cards get a quick confirmation. Borderline or valuable cards go to a senior reviewer.
  5. Learn
    Every human correction is logged to recalibrate the models.
